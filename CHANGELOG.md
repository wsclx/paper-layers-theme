## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-24

### Added
- First release: one theme with light and dark mode.
- Cards as sheets of matte card stock on a petrol board, without outlines; depth only from short layer shadows. Works without add-ons.
- Fine paper fibre as an embedded SVG on cards and board, no image files.
- Mustard for lights, coral for heating and media, petrol for actions and selection; in light mode mustard only as a surface, icons and chart lines in a darker ochre (3:1 on the sheet); darker text variants for error, warning and success.
- `rgb-*` partners for the card and page background, so translucent overlays use the card stock colours.
- Paper switch thumbs on a petrol track, rounded system font for text and headings.
- Optional card-mod rules: switched-on tiles lifted one layer higher (in dark mode also a lighter sheet) with a solid paper dot behind the icon, the sidebar as a floating sheet with a raised selection, rounded heading font for section headings and the dashboard title. The lift does not animate when reduced motion is requested.
